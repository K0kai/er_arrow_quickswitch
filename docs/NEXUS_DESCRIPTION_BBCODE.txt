[center][size=6][b][color=#6d9eeb]Arrow Wheel[/color][/b][/size]
[i]Quick-switch arrows and bolts with radial wheels, no inventory needed[/i]
[/center]

[center][url=https://github.com/K0kai/er_arrow_quickswitch]Source code on GitHub[/url][/center]

[line]

[size=4][b][color=#6d9eeb]What it does[/color][/b][/size]

Hold a key, choose the ammo you want, and release. Arrow Wheel adds [b]four selectors[/b], one for each arrow slot and one for each bolt slot, with optional controller support.

[list]
[*][b]Wheels:[/b] arrow slots 1 and 2 on [b]1[/b] and [b]2[/b], bolt slots 1 and 2 on [b]3[/b] and [b]4[/b] (rebindable).[/*]
[*][b]What's shown:[/b] only ammo you actually own. Arrow wheels list arrows and greatarrows, bolt wheels list bolts and ballista bolts. Empty stacks and ammo already equipped in the paired slot are hidden.[/*]
[*][b]Info on each wedge:[/b] name in your game language, quantity, item artwork, and damage/status badges. Multiple effects can appear together.[/*]
[*][b]Many arrows?[/b] Long lists are split into pages. Scroll to change pages on the radial wheel or step through items in the strip and list.[/*]
[*][b]Works with modded ammo:[/b] names and effects are read from the running game, so there are no fixed ammo ID lists and no Convergence-specific tables.[/*]
[*][b]Controller (optional):[/b] hold a bound button, aim with the right stick on the radial wheel, or step through strip/list items with [b]RB[/b]/[b]LB[/b]. Press [b]Back+Start[/b] to open or close settings.[/*]
[*][b]Selection styles:[/b] use the radial wheel, a horizontal strip, a side list, or a quick strip. The strip and list use scrolling or controller page buttons to move through ammo.[/*]
[*][b]Optional tap and hold:[/b] use a short press to cycle ammo and a hold to open the selector.[/*]
[*][b]Camera control:[/b] the radial wheel keeps the camera still while the mouse chooses ammo by default; this can be disabled.[/*]
[*][b]Clearer display:[/b] improved text rendering, smoother artwork, and adjustable contrast and colors.[/*]
[/list]

[list=1]

[*]Extract the download anywhere you like and keep this layout. If an [font=Courier New]assets[/font] folder is included, keep it next to [font=Courier New]dist[/font] for effect badges.
[code]ArrowQuickSwitch/

    ArrowQuickSwitch.dll
  assets/

[/*]

[size=4][b][color=#6d9eeb]Configuration[/color][/b][/size]

Press [b]F10[/b] or [b]Back+Start[/b] on a controller during gameplay to open the settings panel, powered by [b]Dear ImGui[/b]. Open a wheel once to populate its preview.

[list]
[*][b]Wheel and indicator:[/b] choose a selection style, camera behavior, colors, transparency, size, position, text, icons, selection lighting, and decorative trim. The ammo indicator can also be turned off.[/*]
[*][b]Controls:[/b] change keyboard and controller bindings for all four wheels, controller paging, and the settings panel. Click a binding and press the desired key or mouse button; hold controller buttons together and release to capture a chord. Escape cancels. Manual name entry is also available. Keyboard chords such as [font=Courier New]Ctrl+E[/font] and controller chords such as [font=Courier New]LT+DPadUp[/font] are supported.[/*]
[*][b]Behavior:[/b] toggle sounds, use optional tap and hold cycling, and restrict each selector to its equipped ammo type, if preferred. An empty slot ignores this restriction for its first selection.[/*]
[/list]

Appearance changes preview immediately. Save to keep them and apply controls and behavior without restarting. Controller buttons still reach the game, so choose bindings that avoid gameplay conflicts.

Settings are saved in [font=Courier New]ArrowQuickSwitch.ini[/font]. See the included [font=Courier New]CUSTOMIZATION.md[/font] for all available options.

Arrow and bolt artwork is loaded from installed game/mod files at runtime. Missing artwork falls back to effect badges.
